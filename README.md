# SQL E-Commerce Queries

## About the Project
Analyzing an E-Commerce dataset from Kaggle to answer business questions about sales trends, customer behavior, and payment patterns using MySQL.

## Business Questions
### Customer Segmentation & Discounts
- Which customer segments receive the most discounts?
- Which payment method is most popular for new customers?
- Which payment method is most popular for each age group?
- What category is most popular for each age group?
- What is the average order value by customer segment?

### Payments
- Which payment method is most popular overall?
- Which payment method is most popular for each age group?

### Returns
- How many items are returned in total?
- Which products are returned most often?
- How many items are returned by new customers vs. VIP customers?

### Products
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- What are the top-performing product categories by revenue?
- What is the most popular product overall?
